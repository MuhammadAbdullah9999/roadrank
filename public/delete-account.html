Delete your Road Rank account

Last updated: October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Road Rank and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete data / account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete account & device data.

This permanently removes your account, leaderboard profile, club memberships, cloud backup, submitted drive records and Pro entitlement records from our servers, and the trips stored on your phone.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email hafizzabdullah999@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm once it is done.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.
  • Deletion cannot be undone. Export your data first in Settings, Export my personal data, if you want a copy.
