Every quick acceleration costs 3 points in Road Rank. It also tends to cost fuel, tyres and calm.

## What counts

A quick acceleration is a sharp increase in speed over a short time, such as launching from a light or flooring it to merge.

## Why we do it

- To "beat" the car next to us at the lights
- To catch a gap in traffic
- Habit, especially in cars with a lot of power
- Impatience when running late

## How to accelerate smoothly

1. **Press, do not stamp.** Squeeze the pedal progressively.
2. **Shift early in a manual.** Do not rev high in each gear.
3. **Use the road ahead.** If the light is red 200 metres away, there is no reason to sprint.
4. **Merge with planning.** Match speed gradually on slip roads instead of a late burst.
5. **Take it easy in electric cars.** Instant torque makes it easy to accelerate faster than you intend.

## Why it matters beyond the score

Heavy acceleration uses more energy in any type of vehicle. Smooth acceleration is also easier on tyres, and it makes passengers more comfortable.

## Be realistic

You will still need to accelerate decisively at times, such as joining a fast road. The aim is to avoid unnecessary harsh pull-aways in everyday driving.

## Track your progress

Check your trip breakdown. If your acceleration count falls over a few weeks, your habits are changing.
