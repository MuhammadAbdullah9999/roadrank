The world leaderboard is fun, but most people care about beating a sibling, a coworker or a friend. Private clubs make that easy.

## What is a club?

A club is a private group joined with an invite code. Members can compare driving scores with each other. Road Rank Pro lets you create clubs for up to 50 friends.

## What members can see

Members see your name, your drive count and your average score. Routes and email stay private. Clubs are about scores, not speed rankings, so they do not reward going faster.

## Setting one up

1. Create a club in the app with Pro.
2. Share the invite code with the people you want.
3. Everyone drives as normal and scores update.
4. Check the club board to see where everyone stands.

## Ideas for friendly competitions

- **Family challenge:** who has the best average this month?
- **Commuter league:** coworkers on similar routes compare scores.
- **New driver mentor:** a parent and a new driver agree on a target score together.
- **Road trip duel:** friends on the same trip see who stays smoothest.

## Keep it positive

Reward improvement as well as the top score. Celebrate the person who raised their average the most. Never pressure anyone to chase a score in a way that makes them drive unsafely or use their phone.

## Prefer to stay private?

You do not have to join any club or leaderboard. Road Rank works on its own as a personal driving journal.
