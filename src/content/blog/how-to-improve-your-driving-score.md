---
title: "7 habits that improve your driving score"
description: "Simple, safe habits that make your driving smoother and lift your score trip after trip."
date: 2026-10-14
---
A higher driving score mostly comes from small habits. None of them need special skill.

## 1. Look further ahead

Scan the road well past the car in front. Early awareness turns hard braking into gentle slowing.

## 2. Keep a gap

A bigger gap gives you time to react smoothly. Extra space is the single biggest help.

## 3. Accelerate gently

Press the pedal progressively. You will still get there at nearly the same time.

## 4. Brake early and lightly

Start slowing sooner and ease off the brake near the end instead of stopping abruptly.

## 5. Take corners slowly

Slow down before the turn. Hold a steady speed through it.

## 6. Plan before you go

Set your route and start the app before you set off, or have a passenger do it. Never use your phone while driving.

## 7. Review, then repeat

Look at the breakdown after each drive. Pick one thing to improve on the next trip. Small changes add up across a week.
