Driving tracker apps range from simple trip loggers to insurance programmes that watch every move. Before you install one, it is worth knowing what to check. Here is the checklist we used when building Road Rank.

## 1. Does it need extra hardware?

Some apps need a plug-in device or a tag in the car. Others, like Road Rank, use only the phone's GPS and motion sensors. Phone-only apps are easier to start with, though readings depend on how the phone is mounted.

## 2. Is the score explained?

A score is only useful if you know what moves it. Look for an app that shows exactly which events cost points and where. Road Rank starts each drive at 100, takes off points for hard braking and quick acceleration, and shows the breakdown. Read [how the driving score works](/blog/how-driving-score-works).

## 3. When does it collect location?

This is the big privacy question. Some apps track location all the time to detect drives automatically. Others record only the drives you start. Check the app's permissions and privacy policy. Road Rank records only drives you start, and shows a notification for as long as a drive is recording.

## 4. Who can see your data?

Find out whether your trips are shared with insurers, advertisers or other users. On Road Rank, the leaderboard is optional and shows only a display name, location you choose, score and distance. Routes and email are never shown. See [what Road Rank collects](/blog/driving-data-privacy-what-road-rank-collects).

## 5. Can you export and delete your data?

Good apps let you take your data with you and remove it when you leave. Look for exports (such as CSV or GPX) and a clear way to delete your account.

## 6. Battery use

GPS uses power. Apps that track all the time drain more battery than apps that record only during drives. For long trips, a car charger helps either way.

## 7. Is it fun enough to keep using?

The best app is the one you keep opening. Leaderboards, clubs with friends, streaks and trends turn better driving into something you look forward to.

## 8. What does it cost?

Check what is free and what is paid. Road Rank records and scores drives for free. Pro adds the AI coach, HUD mode, cloud backup and exports.

## 9. Does it put safety first?

No app should encourage you to touch your phone while driving. Look for one-tap start before you set off and reviewing your score after you park.
